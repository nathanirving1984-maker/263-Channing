About This Home

Welcome to 263 Channing Way, where generous square footage, light-filled spaces and low-maintenance Marin living come together. Nearly 2,000 square feet across several levels give you room for everyday living, working from home and easy entertaining, with three bedrooms and three full bathrooms.

The open living room has high ceilings, a wall of windows and a wood-burning fireplace, and it flows into the dining area and kitchen, where a breakfast bar makes a natural gathering spot. Off the living room, a balcony looks out over the trees.

The spacious primary suite has a large walk-in closet, an ensuite bath and a private deck with views of the hills to the west, perfect for sunsets. A skylit loft works well as a home office, and on the ground floor a large two-car attached garage adds plenty of storage.

North San Rafael

Part of the Pinnacle condominium community off Professional Center Parkway in the hills of north San Rafael, with green hillside views from the decks. The home is served by the Miller Creek Elementary School District and the San Rafael City High School District, and it’s a short drive to Terra Linda, Northgate shopping and Highway 101.
