About This Home

Welcome to 263 Channing Way, where generous square footage, light-filled spaces, and low-maintenance Marin living come together seamlessly. Offering nearly 2,000 square feet of thoughtful living space, this multi-level home provides the ideal setup for comfortable daily living, working from home, and effortless entertaining. The home features three bedrooms and three full bathrooms. The open living room has high ceilings with abundant natural light and flows into the dining and kitchen areas. The spacious primary suite includes a large walk-in closet, ensuite bath and private deck with views of the hills to the west, perfect for taking in the sunset. On the ground floor is a large two-car attached garage with plenty of storage space.

North San Rafael

Part of the Pinnacle condominium community off Professional Center Parkway in north San Rafael’s hills, with green hillside views from the decks. The home is served by the Miller Creek Elementary School District and the San Rafael City High School District, and sits a short drive from Terra Linda, Northgate shopping, and Highway 101.
